Structured Adaptation

Most businesses sense the shift. Few have a disciplined way to respond. The work begins with a clear look at your current position, the priorities that still matter, how work is organized, and the metrics you use to steer—then produces practical recommendations. Some involve technology. Some do not. The goal is deliberate adaptation, not tool chasing.

1. Assess

Holistic view of SWOT, your “why,” strategic imperatives, current metrics, and organizational realities.

2. Prioritize

Clear recommendations ranked by impact and fit. AI appears only where it serves a specific priority.

3. Adapt

Coaching for leadership decisions and selective implementation of the systems or changes that matter.

About

Reclaim works with Northern California owners and leaders who need a clear, disciplined response to an environment AI has changed. The work draws on more than 25 years translating complex technology into markets, revenue, and customer value—across networking, cloud, SaaS, and cybersecurity—plus hands-on organizational design at scale.

Publicly recognized track record includes leading Juniper Networks Australia & New Zealand for nearly a decade (scaling revenue from $8M to $120M and establishing it as the company’s leading region), managing the highest-growth teams at Juniper, and being recruited by Big Switch Networks as Vice President of Worldwide Sales to accelerate global SDN adoption. Engineering training underpins practical system design; large-scale leadership and organization-design work informs how recommendations are sequenced so they stick. Based in the East Bay. Focused on businesses that want clarity and selective action rather than another set of tools.
