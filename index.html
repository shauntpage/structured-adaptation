Structured Adaptation

Most businesses sense the shift. Few have a disciplined way to respond. The work begins with a clear look at your current position, the priorities that still matter, how work is organized, and the metrics you use to steer—then produces practical recommendations. Some involve technology. Some do not. The goal is deliberate adaptation, not tool chasing.

1. Assess

Holistic view of SWOT, your “why,” strategic imperatives, current metrics, and organizational realities.

2. Prioritize

Clear recommendations ranked by impact and fit. AI appears only where it serves a specific priority.

3. Adapt

Coaching for leadership decisions and selective implementation of the systems or changes that matter.

About

Structured Adapt works with Northern California owners and leaders who need a clear, disciplined response to an environment AI has changed. The work draws on more than 25 years translating complex technology into markets, revenue, and customer value—across networking, cloud, SaaS, and cybersecurity—plus hands-on organizational design at scale.

Experience includes leading global organizations of 500+ people with $800M+ in annual revenue responsibility, scaling a regional business from $8M to $120M, building go-to-market capability from the ground up, and founding AI-focused ventures (including Reclaim). Engineering training underpins practical system design; leadership and organization-design work informs how recommendations are sequenced so they stick.

Based in the East Bay. Focused on businesses that want clarity and selective action rather than another set of tools.
